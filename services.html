How it works

Four steps, start to placement.

The order matters — each stage narrows the field until the right person is the only one left.

01 / Brief

Scope the role

We sit with the hiring manager to define the must-haves, the deal-breakers, and what success looks like at 90 days.

02 / Source

Build the shortlist

We pull from our active network and run targeted outreach until we have a shortlist worth your time.

03 / Screen

Verify the fit

Skills checks, reference calls, and a structured interview — so every candidate you meet has already cleared the bar.

04 / Place

Onboard & support

We handle offer logistics and stay in touch through the first weeks to make sure the placement is actually working.
